# End-User Manual

## 1. Analyzing Audio Files
1. Navigate to **Upload Studio** (`/upload`).
2. Drag & drop your WAV, MP3, AAC, FLAC, or OGG audio file.
3. Click **Execute Forensic Analysis**.
4. Inspect the Waveform, Spectrogram, MFCC Matrix, Pitch Graph, Confidence Meter, and Threat Timeline.

## 2. Real-Time Microphone Streaming
1. Navigate to **Live Mic Stream** (`/realtime`).
2. Click **Start Live Mic Detection** and grant browser microphone access.
3. Speak into the microphone to monitor real-time threat scores and instant alerts.

## 3. Exporting PDF Reports
1. Navigate to **PDF Reports** (`/reports`).
2. Click **Export PDF Forensic Certificate** to save a downloadable PDF report.
